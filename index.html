Origin & Philosophy

From a Compact Camera to the Streets

My photographic journey started early in childhood, holding a small compact camera alongside my grandfather. Walking through unfamiliar corners together taught me to observe nuance—how light fractures across concrete, how shadows shift, and how ordinary frames shelter extraordinary stillness.

As that curiosity matured into a deep commitment to street and documentary photography, the camera evolved into an analytical instrument. This platform is a curated exhibition of my works and a repository of essential optical fundamentals, post-processing techniques, and artistic perspectives.
